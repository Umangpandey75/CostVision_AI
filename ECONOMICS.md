# Unit Economics for Credex

## Lead Value Calculation

**Assumptions:**
- Average startup AI spend: $2,000/month
- Credex discount: 30% on credits
- Gross margin on credits: 15% after provider costs
- Credex revenue per customer: $2,000 * 30% discount * 15% margin = $90/month
- Average customer lifespan: 18 months
- **LTV = $90 * 18 = $1,620 per converted customer**
