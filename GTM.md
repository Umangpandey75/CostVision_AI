# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target User Persona

**"Alex, 32, CTO at a Series A startup (15 engineers)"**
- Signs off on $5k+/month AWS + AI tool bills
- Hangs out on Hacker News, r/startups, Indie Hackers
- Googles "how to reduce cloud costs" and "AI tool comparison"
- Has been burned by vendor lock-in before

## Where They Hang Out

- **Reddit:** r/SaaS, r/startups, r/ExperiencedDevs
- **Slack:** Swey (founder community), Latent Space (AI engineers)
- **Twitter/X:** Follows @levelsio, @shl, @garrytan
- **Newsletters:** TLDR AI, The Pragmatic Engineer, Lenny's Newsletter

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

### Week 1: Direct Outreach (20 users)
- Manual outreach to 50 YC founders on X (DM personalized audit)
- Post in 5 Indie Hackers threads about reducing burn
- Comment on Hacker News "Ask HN: How to reduce SaaS costs?"

### Week 2: Community Launch (50 users)
- Launch on Product Hunt (timed with Tuesday 12am PST)
- Post "Show HN" with demo video
- Share in 10 Slack communities with "built this to solve my own problem"

### Week 3: Viral Loop (30 users)
- Make shareable results highly visual (savings badge)
- Add "Compare with peers" (anonymous benchmark)
- Tweet thread: "I audited 100 startups' AI spend - here's what I found"

## Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex's existing relationships with 50+ funded startups who already buy discounted credits. Offer them free audits in exchange for:
- LinkedIn testimonial
- Case study on their blog
- Intro to 3 other founders

## Week 1 Traction Metrics

**Good:** 100 audits, 30 email captures, 5 consultation requests
**Great:** 500 audits, 150 leads, 10 consultations, 1 viral tweet
**Amazing:** 2,000 audits, featured on Hacker News front page, 20 consultations
