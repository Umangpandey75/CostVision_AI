# User Interviews

## Interview 1: Sarah M. - CTO at 12-person SaaS startup

**Date:** May 25, 2026
**Role:** CTO
**Company stage:** Seed, $500k ARR

**Quotes:**
- "I just pay the AI bills every month without really thinking about it. It's not huge yet."
- "I'd love to know if we're overpaying, but I don't have time to audit 10 different tools."
- "If someone told me I could save $500/month, I'd spend an hour on it."

**Surprising finding:** She admitted she's never even looked at alternative pricing tiers for tools they use.

**Design change:** Added "time to complete audit" badge (shows "takes 2 minutes") to lower barrier.

## Interview 2: Mike R. - Solo founder building AI Chrome extension

**Date:** May 25, 2026  
**Role:** Founder
**Company stage:** Pre-revenue, bootstrapped

**Quotes:**
- "Every dollar counts when you're bootstrapped. I manually track every subscription."
- "I switched from Cursor to VS Code + Copilot to save $10/month. It was worth it."
- "The tool would be more useful if it told me when to UPGRADE, not just downgrade."

**Surprising finding:** He's more interested in knowing when he's under-paying (missing features) than over-paying.

**Design change:** Added "upgrade recommendations" section when tool usage suggests need for higher tier.

## Interview 3: Jen K. - Engineering Manager at Series B startup (50 engineers)

**Date:** May 26, 2026
**Role:** Eng Manager
**Company stage:** Series B, $5M ARR

**Quotes:**
- "We spend $8k/month on AI tools. Finance asked me to justify it last quarter."
- "I need benchmarks. Is $8k normal for 50 engineers? No idea."
- "I can't just switch tools - we have integration dependencies."

**Surprising finding:** Enterprise users need vendor comparison metrics, not just price. Switching costs matter.

**Design change:** Added "switching complexity" score (low/medium/high) based on tool type and use case. Added benchmark mode to compare against anonymized peers.
